# 📊 Online Retail Business Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze online retail business performance across sales, customers, products, countries, and customer retention.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project transforms online retail transaction data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics and helps identify patterns in revenue, customer behavior, product performance, country-wise sales, RFM segments, and cancelled products.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor overall retail business performance
- Analyze revenue and order trends
- Identify high-performing countries
- Understand customer types and retention
- Analyze RFM customer segments
- Identify high-revenue products
- Monitor products with high cancellation volumes
- Provide actionable business insights through interactive visualizations

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The United Kingdom contributes the largest share of revenue, accounting for approximately 86.2% of the displayed country revenue.
- Repeat customers represent 67.03% of the customer distribution, compared with 32.97% one-time customers.
- The Champions RFM segment contributes approximately 5.6M in revenue.
- Revenue varies considerably across months, with a visible increase toward the later part of the year.
- Revenue is highly concentrated among the leading country compared with other markets.
- A relatively small group of products contributes a significant portion of product-level revenue.
- Certain products show substantially higher cancellation volumes and may require further investigation.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus retention initiatives on repeat and high-value customers.
- Investigate products with unusually high cancellation volumes.
- Use seasonal revenue patterns for inventory and promotional planning.
- Monitor country-level concentration and explore opportunities for market diversification.
- Prioritize high-revenue products in inventory and marketing analysis.
